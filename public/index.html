Already paying someone?

"But I already pay £300 a month for social."

Most owners we meet already are. And most of them can't tell us a single customer it brought in. That isn't a bargain. It's £3,600 a year buying nothing.

Cheap social media isn't cheap. It's just small enough that you stop questioning it.

The problem usually isn't the person. It's the brief. £300 a month buys someone posting at your audience: a few reels cut from photos you sent, with no offer behind them, no ad spend, nowhere for the viewer to go and no way of knowing whether any of it worked.

The views look like activity. The till doesn't move. So before you compare us on price, compare us on what the money actually buys.

About SAI Social

We don't sell impressions. We sell customers.

We're a small performance marketing team running social, web and paid ads for local businesses and online brands. Everything we build is engineered to one metric that matters: how many people actually bought.

Our story

Built on the floor, not in a boardroom.

SAI Social started because too many businesses were paying for "reach" and "engagement" while their Tuesdays stayed empty and their carts stayed abandoned. Some were paying an agency thousands. More were paying a few hundred a month to someone who posted at their audience and called it marketing. Neither group could name a single customer it brought them.

So we built the thing we'd want to buy. We write your real numbers down before we touch anything. We film on site, because you can't sell a room you've never stood in. And every campaign is judged against that day-one baseline, in plain figures, every week.

Today we run three pillars (social, web and paid ads) for restaurants, bars, takeaways, salons, barbershops, e-commerce businesses and personal brands. Most of our work is in those trades, but we're open to any business that needs more customers.

What we believe

Four things we won't be talked out of.

01

Write the number down first

"We grew your account" is worthless. "Wednesday covers went 34 to 91 in eight weeks" is worth a fortune, and you only get to say it if someone wrote down 34 on day one. It's why the Baseline Guarantee exists.

02

Film it in the room

Content made from photos you emailed to someone who's never been in your building looks exactly like what it is. We turn up, we shoot, we know what your product and your people actually look like.

03

Views are not the job

A video with 200,000 views and no bookings is a hobby. We'll take the reel that got 4,000 views and filled a Tuesday, every time. Impressions are the easiest number in marketing to move and the only one that never reached a till.

04

Show the working

Real prices on the pricing page. Plain-number reporting, weekly. No invented case studies, no borrowed logos, and no results claimed that we can't show you the tracking for.
