# Pricing: SAI Social

Last updated: 5 August 2026 · Currency: GBP (£) · Country: United Kingdom

SAI Social is a UK marketing agency running three services together on one
monthly price: social (short-form video), web (site, SEO, email) and paid ads.
There is no setup fee and no minimum contract length.

- Price: £800/month
- Billing: monthly, no setup fee, no minimum term
- Content:
  - 8 short-form videos per month, filmed and edited by us
  - On-site shoot day at the client's premises, no stock footage
  - Cut for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts from one shoot
  - Captions burned in as standard
  - Licensed trending audio, so posts are not muted or struck
  - Cover frame designed for every video
  - Hooks written and tested
  - Client approves everything before it is published
- Profile and community:
  - Bio, links and highlights rebuilt to convert
  - Grid kept visually consistent
  - Comments, DMs and replies handled daily
  - Review responses drafted for the client
- Strategy and reporting:
  - Competitor and market research before filming
  - Content calendar planned a month ahead
  - Posting timed to the audience's active hours
  - Monthly performance report in plain numbers
  - Baseline recorded in writing before work starts
  - The Baseline Guarantee
  - Direct contact with the people doing the work, not an account manager
- Does not include: paid ad management, website build, SEO
- Media budget required: none. Bronze runs no paid advertising

## The Baseline Guarantee

Included on every tier at no extra cost.

We record the client's real numbers (bookings, orders, enquiries, revenue)
in writing before any work begins. If those numbers have not moved after 90
days, month four is free.

We do not guarantee impressions, views or follower counts.

Silver covers the same scope at £1,500/month. A full-time UK social media
manager costs roughly £3,000/month once employer's NI and pension are added,
and that is one person without the software, camera or ad management.

These figures are estimates for comparison. Actual quotes vary.
